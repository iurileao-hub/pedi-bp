## Why do our parameters differ slightly from pedbp?

The pedbp R package uses `optim()` with L-BFGS-B method, while we use `numpy.linalg.lstsq` (exact linear regression). Both methods solve the same optimization problem (minimizing squared error of BP = mu + sigma * z), but:

1. **Different optimizer implementations** - R's `optim(L-BFGS-B)` is an iterative numerical optimizer; our `lstsq` gives the exact analytical solution to the linear regression.
2. **Floating-point differences** - Different languages/libraries have slightly different floating-point behavior.
3. **Both are valid** - The cross-validation section below confirms that our lstsq and Nelder-Mead give identical results (diff = 0.000000), proving our solution is correct.

The key metric is **reconstruction error**: how well the fitted parameters reproduce the original published table values. Our NHLBI parameters achieve perfect reconstruction (all within 0.64 mmHg), and our Flynn 2017 parameters are within 1.22 mmHg (with only 5/714 checks exceeding 1 mmHg).
